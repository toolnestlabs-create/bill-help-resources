# Bill Help Resources

A plain-language list of ways to get help paying rent, utilities, phone and other bills in the United States.

Where to start: dial 211 or visit 211.org for local rent and utility help. For heating and cooling bills, look up LIHEAP in your state. Many utilities also run hardship funds and payment plans.

Guides and a step-by-step bill help finder: https://boredbillionairebill.com/

Maintained by Bored Billionaire Bill.
